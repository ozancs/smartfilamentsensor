Smart Filament Sensor

Official Bill of Materials (BOM) & Component Specifications

Image Category Component Name Qty Description / Technical Details
Electronics ESP32-C3 Super Mini 1 Main microcontroller. WiFi/BLE enabled, ultra-compact form factor perfect for tight enclosures.
Electronics AS5600 Encoder Module 1 Contactless magnetic rotary encoder. Must include a diametrical magnet for proper tracking.
Electronics WS2812B NeoPixel 5050 1 RGB status indicator LED mounted on a single round breakout PCB.
Mechanical Grooved Bearing (U604ZZ) 2 OD: 13mm, ID: 4mm. The central pulley/bearing that the filament rides on.
Mechanical Dowel Pin (3mm x 15mm) 1 Smooth hardened steel pin used as the main axle/shaft for the bearing.
Mechanical O-Ring / Seal Ring 1 OD: ~11mm. Stretched over the grooved bearing to grip the filament securely.
Hardware PC4-M6 Fitting 2 6mm thread pneumatic connectors used as entry and exit guides for Bowden/PTFE tubes.
Hardware Compression Spring 1 Pen-style spring to maintain tension. Can be cut to length during final assembly.
Hardware M3 Screw 1 M8x8 - M3x10 is enough
Hardware M2 Self Tapping Screw 1 Max 7mm deep for as5600, Max 4mm for securing esp32
